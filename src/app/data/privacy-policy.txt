Section 3. Purpose of Data Collection
Charming Fur-fection Pet Care Services uses the information collected through its web-based
booking management system for the following purposes:
3.1. Account Management
To create and manage customer accounts, authenticate users, maintain account
information, and enforce account registration limits intended to help prevent fraudulent
activity.
3.2. Pet Profile Management
To allow customers to create and update pet profiles containing relevant information,
such as pet names, breeds, ages, weights, photographs, and health notes, to support the
delivery of the appropriate pet care services.
3.3. Appointment Management
To process appointment requests, manage booking schedules, monitor appointment
statuses, prevent scheduling conflicts. The system also provides customers with
appointment updates, including approvals, cancellations, rejections, and other relevant
booking changes through the notification box within the website, and an appointment
reminder sent through email notifications an hour before the appointment.
3.4. Customer Support and Communication
To respond to customer inquiries, provide information about available services and
business policies, and communicate information relevant to appointments and the use of
the system. Customer communications may be provided through the website’s
notification feature, email, or other available communication channels.
3.5. AI Chatbot Assistance

To process customer inquiries through the AI-powered chatbot and generate responses
concerning general service information, booking procedures, business policies, and
general pet health and safety information. The chatbot may also direct customers to the
appointment booking page when further assistance is needed.
3.6. Fraud Detection and System Security
To use recorded IP addresses to enforce the limit of three (3) accounts per IP address and
help identify or prevent potentially fraudulent account creation.
3.7. System Operation and Improvement
To support the proper operation of the booking management system, identify technical
issues, and evaluate system performance where applicable.
3.8. No-Show Monitoring and Account Restrictions
The system monitors the status of the appointments to identify the appointments that have
not been marked as completed by the end of the scheduled day. These appointments will
be automatically marked as no-show in the system.
The system keeps count of no-show records associated with customer accounts in order
to monitor repeated missed appointments. When a customer accumulates three (3)
no-shows, the system automatically suspends the customer’s account for three (3) days.
During the suspension period, the customer cannot book appointments.
This information is processed to manage appointment attendance, enforce booking
policies, and support the proper operation of the appointment system.
Information will be used only for purposes relevant to the operation of the system and the
provision of pet care services, subject to applicable data privacy requirements. If information is
needed for a new purpose that is not compatible with the original purpose of collection, the
business will address the legal requirements before processing it for that purpose.
