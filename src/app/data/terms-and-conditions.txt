Section 5. Appointment Cancellation, Rejection, Expiration, and No-Shows
Customers must follow the business’ applicable appointment cancellation policy. Any
cancellation deadline or restriction must be communicated to customers before it applies to their
bookings.
‘The business may reject a booking request when the requested service cannot be
accommodated, staff or clinic capacity is insufficient, a required professional is unavailable, or
the pet’s disclosed health information indicates that the service requested may be unsuitable.

When an appointment is rejected or cancelled, the business will provide the applicable status
update and reason through the system.
Appointments will be marked as expired when the customer who made an appointment did not
show up for their scheduled date and time.
Failure to attend an appointment without prior cancellation may result in the appointment being
recorded as no-show. A total of three (3) no-shows will result in a three-day account suspension.
Section 6. No-Show Policy and Temporary Account Suspension
Customers are expected to attend their scheduled appointments. At the end of each scheduled
day, appointments that have not been marked as completed in the system will automatically be
marked as no-show.
When a customer accumulates three (3) no-shows, their account will be automatically suspended
for three (3) days. During this period, the customer will be unable to book appointments.
Customers are encouraged to contact Charming Fur-fection Pet Care Services if they believe a
no-show status has been recorded incorrectly. The business may review reported concerns and
correct appointment records when appropriate.

Section 8. AI Chatbot Usage
The AI-powered chatbot is provided to assist users with inquiries regarding services, schedules,
policies, and booking process of Charming Fur-fection Pet Care Services.
The chatbot supports English and Tagalog inquiries within its intended functionality. Customers

The chatbot is not intended to provide diagnosis, replace professional veterinary advice, or

Customers must exercise caution when following the chatbot’s medical responses, as the
