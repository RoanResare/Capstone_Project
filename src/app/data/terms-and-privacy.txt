‘The business may reject a booking request when the requested service cannot be

When an appointment is rejected or cancelled, the business will provide the applicable status

Failure to attend an appointment without prior cancellation may result in the appointment being

Section 6. No-Show Policy and Temporary Account Suspension
Customers are expected to attend their scheduled appointments. At the end of each scheduled
day, appointments that have not been marked as completed in the system will automatically be
marked as no-show.
When a customer accumulates three (3) no-shows, their account will be automatically suspended
for three (3) days. During this period, the customer will be unable to book appointments.
Customers are encouraged to contact Charming Fur-fection Pet Care Services if they believe a
no-show status has been recorded incorrectly. The business may review reported concerns and
correct appointment records when appropriate.
